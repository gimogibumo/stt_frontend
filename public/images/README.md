# Live atmosphere

`live-atmosphere.jpg` is an AI-generated decorative concert photograph, created
with the built-in imagegen tool. It does not depict a specific advertised concert
or artist and is used only as the generic homepage banner background. Concert
posters are code-generated artwork; titles and dates come from the concert API.

Generation prompt: Wide editorial concert photograph viewed from the back of an
audience, silhouettes along the bottom, orange and amber stage lights on the
right, dark negative space on the left for webpage copy, smoky atmospheric light,
subtle film grain, no identifiable artist, no text, logos, or watermarks.

The generated PNG was converted to JPEG at quality 82 for the website.
